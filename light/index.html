Meet Gene Sheldrick, aka Magik Gee

Magik Gee is the DJ name of Gene Sheldrick, who runs Endorfanize Entertainment. He brings the music, the sound system and the lights, then reads the room to keep the dance floor full until the end.

His sets run from party classics of the 60s to the 90s through to house, bassline, UK garage, jungle and old-school rave, matched to your crowd. In March 2026 he played the 80s Party at The Beech Tree in St Mary Cray, and his mixes are on Mixcloud.

Questions from party hosts

Who is Magik Gee?

Magik Gee is the DJ name of Gene Sheldrick, who runs Endorfanize Entertainment. He DJs birthdays, family celebrations and themed nights across Kent and London.

What parties do you DJ?

Birthday parties, family celebrations such as anniversaries and engagements, and themed nights such as 80s parties and fancy dress.

What music do you play?

House, tech house, bassline, UK garage, jungle and drum and bass, old-school rave, and party classics from the 60s to the 90s. Every set is matched to your crowd, with clean or explicit versions.

Do you bring sound and lights?

Yes. A sound system and party lighting come with every booking, along with set-up and pack-down.

How much does it cost?

Every party gets its own quote covering music, sound and lights. Send your date, venue and type of event to get one.

Where do you play?

Across Kent and London.

How do I book?

Use the form on this site, email endorfanizeentertainment@outlook.com, or message 07594 601259 on WhatsApp with your date, venue and type of event. Gene checks the diary and sends a quote.

Can we make requests?

Yes. Requests are welcome on the night.
